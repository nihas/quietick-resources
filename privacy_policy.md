# Privacy Policy for Quietick: Clock & Focus

**Last updated: September 17, 2026**

Quietick: Clock & Focus ("Quietick", "the app", "we", "our", or "us") is a clock, focus timer, and Pomodoro application designed to help users manage their time and focus.

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Quietick.

## 1. Information We Collect

The current version of Quietick does **not collect, transmit, or store personal information on external servers**.

You are not required to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location to use the app.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

Quietick may store certain app preferences locally on your device so that the app can remember your settings.

This may include preferences such as:

* Selected clock style
* Selected colors or themes
* Timer and Pomodoro preferences
* Other app configuration settings

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Removing the app or clearing its application data may remove these locally stored preferences.

## 3. Advertising

The current version of Quietick does **not display advertisements** and does not include advertising functionality.

If advertisements are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the advertising services used and any information that may be processed by those services.

## 4. Analytics and Tracking

The current version of Quietick does not use advertising trackers or analytics services to track users across apps or websites.

If analytics or similar services are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The current version of Quietick does not intentionally send personal information to third-party services for advertising or marketing purposes.

Future versions of the app may integrate third-party services. If such services process user information, this Privacy Policy will be updated to identify the relevant practices.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information collected through Quietick because the current version of the app does not collect personal information from its users.

## 7. Data Security

Quietick is designed to minimize the collection of user information. App preferences used by the current version are stored locally on the user's device.

Please note that no device or software environment can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Quietick is a general-purpose clock and productivity application and is not specifically designed or directed toward children under the age of 13.

The current version of the app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Quietick may receive new features and functionality over time.

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's functionality or data practices change, including if we introduce features such as advertising, analytics, cloud synchronization, accounts, or other online services.

Changes will be reflected on this page by updating the "Last updated" date.
